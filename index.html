What's inside

Built for the days you can't start.

Every task app assumes you will start. Progrex assumes you might not.

When you freeze

"Stuck on this?" has an answer.

Tap it and Progrex asks why. Every reason leads to something the app actually does about it.

  • Too big? It helps you name one concrete first step.
  • Too tired? It moves the task to the hour your energy works.
  • Not sure it matters? It shows your own answer back next time.
A two-minute on-ramp

The hardest part is the first two minutes.

Commit to just that. Progrex asks for your first physical move, then keeps the clock while you put the phone down. Stop with no guilt if you want. Most of the time you won’t want to.

  • Name one concrete first move.
  • A short line to help you commit.
Right on your home screen

Your month, at a glance.

The Month widget shows every task on the day it is due. Tap a day to finish or add tasks without opening the app.

  • A second widget lists today.
  • Five looks to choose from.
  • A small card can even ask you for a free moment.
Something to come back to

A little world that grows when you do.

Rex, a small flame, cheers your first win. Sprout grows from a seed as you finish tasks, and in the tank your finished tasks become fish.

  • Rex can give an optional daily nudge to start, off by default.
  • Streaks and badges that only move with real work.
And there's more

Everything else you'd want.

Small things that make it easier to come back tomorrow.

  • Task wheel: can't choose? Spin it.
  • Treat wheel: win a treat for your effort.
  • Badges, from bronze all the way to diamond.
  • Routines: small steps, every day.
PeersHand a task to a friend. Only they can mark it done.
