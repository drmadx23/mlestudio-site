Medrex Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

1Who we are

Medrex is a medicine reminder app made by MLE Studio (ABDULHADY MOHAMMED JAHHAF Establishment For innovative solutions). You can write to us at MLE.studio@hotmail.com.

2What stays on your phone

Medicine names, shapes, colours, dose times, notes, stock counts, your dose history, visits, your emergency card, care routines and photos are saved on your phone only. We do not receive them unless you turn on family sharing.

3What goes online, only if you share

If you sign in with Google (needed only for the family link), your name and email are stored with Google Firebase Authentication.

If you let a family member follow you, the following is stored online, in Google Firebase and Cloudflare, so that the people you allowed can see it:

  • your doses: the medicine name, the time, and whether it was taken, missed or skipped
  • your visits: the doctor, day, time and reason
  • medicine photos you add
  • the names of the people linked and the change requests they send
  • a notification token, so a linked phone can be alerted

Only the people you allow can see this.

4Permissions

Medrex asks for:

  • notifications and exact alarms, so reminders ring on time
  • display over other apps and full-screen alerts, to show the dose alert
  • vibration
  • start after restart, so reminders return after the phone restarts
  • internet, used by the family link only
  • camera, only when you take a photo

5Stopping and deleting

When you stop sharing, your shared doses, visits, followers, requests and photos are deleted from our servers. Removing the app deletes everything on your phone. To ask us to delete anything else, write to MLE.studio@hotmail.com.

6Who else sees data

We never sell your information. Google (Firebase) and Cloudflare process shared data for us as service providers, and nobody else receives it. Medrex shows no ads and has no analytics today. If that changes, this page changes first.

7Medical notice

Medrex is a reminder. It is not a doctor and gives no medical advice. Follow your doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency call your emergency number.

8Children

Medrex is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9This website

mlestudio.app counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.

10Changes

If we change this policy we change the date at the top, and we tell you in the app if the change matters to you.
